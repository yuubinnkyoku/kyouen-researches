## 3. minpos semantics (implementation-verified)

`--minpos=P` pins the **leftmost stone of the first deficient row at column exactly P**
(sat3.js: `cell[y][P]` unit plus `¬cell[y][x]` for x < P, y = D[0]). The k=5 coverage
argument is: every configuration or its column mirror has leftmost stone column ≤ 10 at
m=22, since x1 ≥ 11 forces x5 ≥ x1 ≥ 11, so the mirror's leftmost column 21−x5 ≤ 10; the
column mirror preserves row occupancy, hence the class and the k-row constraint.
Note (correction of an early draft of this lane): the minpos instances are NOT
min(x1, 21−x5)-equality splits; p=9 and p=10 are **nontrivial** instances at m=22
(60,133 / 47,157 conflicts for {0}; 73,876 / 37,581 for {1}), not vacuous.

## 4. Threshold sharpening

Since every width in {19, 20, 21, 22} is now verified witness-free (K0738–K0759, K0816,
K0821, K0834) and the largest known witness width is 18 (K0332, 29 stones), the threshold
alternative sharpens (K0821-audit convention) to

**M_{5,7} = 19  or  M_{5,7} ≥ 24.**

Reason: M is the least m0 such that all widths ≥ m0 are witness-free. If no witness exists
at any width ≥ 19, then M = 19 (the witness at 18 blocks anything smaller). If a witness
exists at some width w ≥ 23, then M = w + 1 ≥ 24; widths 20–22 cannot be M (they are
verified witness-free), and M = 23 is impossible because it would require a witness at
the verified witness-free width 22. The loose bracket "M ∈ {19,20,21,22} or ≥ 23" in the
report/K-item titles and the CSV row follows the pre-audit convention of K0821's title
and the (6,8) row; this section is the sharpened form. Upper bound 158 (K0332/K0024
substitution) unchanged; exact M needs persistence at 23..157 or a universal tail
argument; the witness-search frontier is 5x23.
