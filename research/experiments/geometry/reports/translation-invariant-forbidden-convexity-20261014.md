# Translation-invariant forbidden sets

For any finite cross-section Y and any translation-invariant family of q-point subsets, the count F(m) in [0,m-1] × Y satisfies F(m+1)-2F(m)+F(m-1)=b_m >= 0, where b_m counts horizontal-translation classes with span m. Proof: each class of span s has exactly max(0,m-s) placements.
